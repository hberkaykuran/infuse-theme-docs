Why Infuse

Small pieces, fast pages

Everything in the theme is tuned for speed first, then dressed up. Your customers get instant pages; you get a storefront that is easy to rearrange.

Built from blocks

Compose pages from small reusable blocks: text, images, buttons, product cards. Arrange them in the editor without touching code.

Fast on purpose

Performance budgets are baked in: tiny deferred scripts, no third-party libraries, and images sized exactly to the layout.

Two presets, one click

Infuse is warm and playful, Utility is dense and engineered. Switch the whole look from a single setting in the editor.

Accessible out of the box

Every color pairing meets WCAG AA in both schemes, down to the small print. Keyboard and screen reader friendly throughout.

Ready for the world

Language and currency pickers come wired for Shopify Markets, and every line of theme copy is translatable.

Deep settings, safe defaults

Every section exposes spacing, color scheme, and layout knobs, and still looks right before you change a thing.
