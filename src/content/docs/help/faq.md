# Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions merchants ask most. If yours is not here, [get in touch](/docs/help/support).

<details>
<summary>How do I install the theme?</summary>

Add Infuse to your theme library from your Shopify admin under Online Store then Themes, then press Customize to open the editor. The full walk-through is on the [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/quick-start) page.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I switch between the Infuse and Heirloom styles?</summary>

Open Theme settings then Design preset and choose one. The change applies across the whole theme. See [Styles](/docs/customization/presets).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I change my logo?</summary>

Select the header in the theme editor and set the Logo image, then adjust the logo width. A favicon is set separately under Theme settings then Layout.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I edit my navigation menu?</summary>

Menus are managed in your Shopify admin under Online Store then Navigation. Edit your menu there, then choose it in the header Menu setting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I change colours?</summary>

Colours live in schemes under Theme settings then Colors. Set your brand colours in the schemes, and each section picks which scheme it uses. See [Theme settings](/docs/customization/theme-settings).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I add my own sections and blocks?</summary>

Yes. Use Add section on a page, then Add block inside a section. The [Sections](/docs/reference/sections) and [Blocks](/docs/reference/blocks) pages list everything available.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the theme translatable?</summary>

Yes. Every theme text can be translated with Shopify's free Translate & Adapt app. See [Localization](/docs/guides/localization).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the theme support multiple currencies?</summary>

Yes, through Shopify Markets. Turn on the country and currency picker in the footer settings once your markets are set up.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will my changes go live immediately?</summary>

No. You edit an unpublished copy and preview it safely. Changes go live only when you press Publish.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I make my product page load quickly?</summary>

Mark one above-the-fold image as the priority image per page, and avoid marking several. The theme keeps scripts small and defers them, so most of the work is already done.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I found a problem. How do I get help?</summary>

Use the [Support](/docs/help/support) page to send us the details, including your store URL and theme version.

</details>
