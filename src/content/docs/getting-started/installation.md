# Add the theme to your store

Add Infuse to your store from the theme package you received. In your Shopify admin, go to *Online Store → Themes*. Your new theme appears in the theme library. Use **Customize** to open the theme editor, where you make all the changes described in the quick start.

While you work, the theme stays unpublished so your live store is untouched. You publish when you are ready.

## Preview before you publish

Use the theme editor's preview to see your changes on a real page. You can also use the **Preview** action in the theme library to open the whole store in a draft state. Nothing you do here affects the published theme until you press **Publish**.

## Publish

When your store looks right, return to the theme library and press **Publish**. Infuse becomes your live theme. You can keep editing afterwards, and your changes save as you go.
