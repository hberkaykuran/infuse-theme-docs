# Localization

Infuse is fully translatable. Every piece of text that comes from the theme can be shown in more than one language, and customers can switch language and region from the footer.

## How translation works

Shopify handles translation for you through the free **Translate & Adapt** app. The theme provides the text; the app stores the translations. You do not edit any theme files.

1. Add the languages you want under *Settings → Languages* in your Shopify admin, and publish them.
2. Install **Translate & Adapt** from the Shopify App Store.
3. Open the app and translate each area: your products and collections, your pages and blog posts, your navigation menus, and the theme's own text.
4. Publish. Customers can now switch language from the picker in the footer.

## Language and country pickers

The footer can show a language picker and a country or region picker. Select the footer in the theme editor to turn these on or off:

- **Show country/region and language picker**: the main toggle for the localization form in the footer.
- **Show country/currency picker**: turn this on only if you sell in more than one currency. The language picker still appears on its own when you publish more than one language.

## Tips

- Translate the section content you typed in the editor as well as your products. Both live in Translate & Adapt.
- When you change a piece of text in the theme editor, revisit its translation so the two stay in step.
- Set the correct default language and currency under *Settings → Languages* and *Settings → Markets*.
